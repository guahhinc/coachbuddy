How to use Coachbuddy

Last Updated: July 14, 2026

Welcome to Coachbuddy. This guide quickly teaches you of how to set up your team, track stats, and make the most of our tools.

First create an account or log into an existing one if already created or given to you by your club

Once past the log in/sign up page, to set up your team click on manage in the top bar.

In the manage tab name your team and choose your sport, click create and start adding players and their info, make sure not to use full names. Use just first names, nicknames, initials, or just last names.

Head over to settings in the top bar and adjust to your liking, after that head back home and lets get started with games.

Click 'Add Default Players' to add your team in, you can add additional players like fill in's on the game tab too

Click start and sub players on and off, track times, track points scored if you enabled it, you can drag and drop players as well and track the whole game with ease.

Once the game is over, click 'Finish Game' to upload the data to the servers to be saved, you can look back at these games in the stats tab at any time.

That's all you need to know about Coachbuddy, discover new features like Guahh AI later and we hope our tools can help even play times for players and help improve time management. Thankyou for using.
